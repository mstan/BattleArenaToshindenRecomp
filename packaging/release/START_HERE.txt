Battle Arena Toshinden Recompiled 0.1.0 - Initial Release

Believed to be stable based on gameplay testing and user validation,
despite being an early release. Bug reports are welcome.

1. Extract the Windows ZIP, or mark the Linux AppImage executable and run it.
2. Select your legally obtained USA BIN/CUE disc image in the launcher.
   Keep all 14 tracks together beside the CUE. No disc data is included.
3. The bundled MIT-licensed OpenBIOS is available by default. You can also
   select a supported retail BIOS dump that you provide.
4. Optional enhancements are on the Mods page and ship disabled.
5. Choose PLAY for local play, or NETPLAY to host/join a two-player session.
   Configure your local controller under PLAYER 1 / NETPLAY. In the game,
   choose VS HUMAN or have P2 press Start. Multitap is not used.

Netplay runs vanilla: all mods are disabled for the session. Two local peers
passed rollback input and state-sync checks; internet matches are not yet
validated. See README.md for tested scope and enhancement controls.

On Windows, keep the package together in a writable folder. Your settings
and saves are local. On Linux, the AppImage creates a writable user data
folder at ~/.local/share/BattleArenaToshindenRecomp (or under XDG_DATA_HOME).
Set BAT_RECOMP_DATA_DIR to override this location.
